1. Installing the app

The app is installed from an APK file (it is not on the Play Store). You only do this once; later versions install over the top and keep your data.
  1. Open the APK download link you were given (GitHub → Actions → the latest green Anjali CRM AI run → Artifacts → anjali-crm-ai-apk). You must be signed in to GitHub to download it.
  2. The download is a .zip. Open it in the Files app and extract the .apk inside.
  3. Tap the .apk. If Android says installing is blocked, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source for your Files app, then go back and tap Install.
  4. If Google Play Protect shows a warning about an unknown developer, tap More details → Install anyway.
  5. Open Anjali CRM AI from your app drawer.
Updating: install the newer APK the same way. Your clients, documents and settings stay on the phone. Take a backup first (Backup and restore) to be safe.

2. First-time setup

Setup wizard
An 8-step wizard that runs the first time you open the app. The counter at the top shows your progress (for example 3/8). Use the back arrow to go back a step.
  1. Profile picture — tap Camera or Gallery, or Skip for now.
  2. What should we call you? — enter your name, tap Continue.
  3. Your mobile number — for example +971 50 123 4567.
  4. Choose your look — Light, Dark or Match my phone.
  5. Create a 6-digit PIN, then enter it again to confirm.
  6. Unlock with your fingerprint — turn on Enable biometric unlock if your phone supports it.
  7. What do you sell? — tick your products (Personal Loan, Business Loan, Mortgage, Credit Card, Auto Loan, Insurance) or tap Add product. At least one is required. Tick Load sample data to get 30 demo leads to practise with. Tap Finish setup.
  8. Save your recovery code — tap Copy and keep it somewhere safe (not only on this phone). Tick I have saved my recovery code and tap Let's go!
The recovery code is the only way back in if you forget your PIN. Write it down.

3. Unlocking the app

Lock screen
The app locks when you leave it for longer than the auto-lock time (5 minutes by default) and every time it starts.

Use pattern / Use PINSwitch between pattern and PIN (only when pattern lock is set).

After unlocking, a short daily motivation message may appear (if turned on). Tap the heart to close it and start your day.

7. Adding and editing a lead

Add Lead
Opened from Add Lead, + Add lead or Edit. Only Full name and Phone are required.

Fast ways to fill it

  • Import contact — pick someone from your phone book. If they have several numbers, choose the Primary phone and WhatsApp number.
  • Scan card — read a business card with the camera (see Scanning a business card).
  • Photo — tap the avatar, then Camera or Gallery.

Fields

  • Contact: Full name*, Phone*, WhatsApp same as phone (untick to type a different WhatsApp number), Email, Location / area, Pick on map.
  • Work & finance: Company, Job title, Nationality, Monthly salary (AED), Requested amount (AED).
  • Lead: Product, Lead source, Status, Priority, Rating (stars), Schedule followup, Remarks.

Tap Save lead (or Save changes). On the Add Lead tab, the eraser icon Clear form starts over.

Duplicates: if the phone number already exists, you'll see Potential Duplicate Customer Found: Merge into the existing record, View Existing Record, Continue Anyway or Cancel.

Header

Name, job and company, status and priority, rating, the lead score (the circle), product, salary and requested/approved/disbursed amounts. Tap the phone icon to call, the WhatsApp icon to message, the email to write a mail, and the location to open the map. A speaker icon reads the remarks aloud.

9. Voice notes and dictation (Notes tab)

Profile — Notes tab
Capture what the client said without typing.
  • Record — records audio. While recording: Pause/Resume, Discard or Save.
  • Dictate — speak and the app types it for you (Listening…). Then Save or Discard.
  • On each note: ▶ play / pause, Edit transcript (or tap the text), and Delete.
Dictation works offline on most phones once the language pack is downloaded in Google's speech settings.

12. Importing files from WhatsApp

Import from WhatsApp
Attach documents a client sent you on WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business in a few taps. Open it from Documents → From WhatsApp or Upload → WhatsApp files.
  1. Allow photo/storage access when asked.
  2. Use the chips to filter: All, WhatsApp Images, WhatsApp Documents, WhatsApp Business Images, WhatsApp Business Documents. The newest files are shown first.
  3. Tick one or more files, then tap Attach N.
  4. Choose the folder (and expiry date if asked).
Can't see a PDF? Newer Android versions only let apps see WhatsApp images. Tap Browse to pick the PDF yourself, or in WhatsApp open the chat, long-press the file, and tap Share → Anjali CRM AI.

13. Calls, followups and files tabs

Calls

Call now dials the client. When you come back to the app it asks How did the call with … go? Pick an outcome, add optional notes, or Skip. Choosing call back asks when, and creates a followup. If a number was flagged as a wrong number, you're warned before calling.

Followups

Add followup → choose date and time → optional note. Each followup's menu has Mark complete (with an optional outcome), Reschedule and Delete. Reminders arrive as notifications (see Notifications).

Files

14. Scanning a business card

Shown until camera access is allowed
Turn a visiting card into a new lead without typing. Open it from Add Lead → Scan card.
  1. Allow camera access when asked (until you do, the screen shows the message pictured; you can still use Gallery).
  2. Point the camera at the card and tap Capture, or tap Gallery to use a photo.
  3. On Review card, check Name, Phone(s), Email, Company and Job title. Use Add phone for another number.
  4. Tap Use details to fill the lead form, or Retake.

The text is read on your phone; nothing is uploaded. If nothing is found, try again in better light.

15. Followups

Followups
Every followup across all clients, grouped so nothing is missed. A red pill shows how many are overdue.
  • Tabs: Today, Tomorrow, This Week, Upcoming, Overdue, Completed.
  • On each card: Call, Reschedule, Mark complete (optional outcome note). The menu has Delete, which also cancels its reminders.
  • + New followup → search and pick a client → date and time → optional note.
You can also tick a followup off directly from Home → Today's tasks.

16. Search

Search
Find any client by name, phone number, company, location, product, status or salary (for example 15000).
  • Type in the box, or tap the mic for Voice search.
  • Recent searches appear as chips; tap one to repeat it.
  • Tap a result to open the client.

17. Reports

Reports
How you are performing over any period.
  • Period dropdown (top right): choose the time range.
  • KPIs: Approval %, Rejection %, Conversion %, Avg followups / lead, Leads, Disbursed.
  • Breakdown: pick a grouping chip (product, source, …) to see Leads vs converted, with a table of Leads, Conv., Rate and Disbursed.
  • Approval conversion gauge and Lead source performance table.
  • Export: Excel (sheets: All Leads, Approved, Rejected, Documents Pending, Followups, Disbursed) or PDF.

18. Picking a location on the map

Save where the client lives or works, so you can navigate there later. Open it from the lead form with Pick on map.
  1. Tap the map or drag the pin. The latitude and longitude are shown.
  2. Current location uses your phone's GPS. Turn on location if asked.
  3. Tap Use this to save the point.

On the client profile, tap the location to open it in your maps app. Remove pin on the form clears it.

19. Messaging on WhatsApp and read-aloud

Tap any WhatsApp icon (or long-press it) to open the WhatsApp · client name sheet:

  • Open chat — opens WhatsApp with the client.
  • Share client details — send the client's details to someone else.
  • Templates — tap a template (★ is your default). The message is filled in with the client's name, product and so on. Edit it in the Message box, then tap Send on WhatsApp.

Read aloud: the speaker buttons read text to you. Tap again to pause or resume, or long-press to stop. A mini player lets you change the Speed.

22. Products

Products
The products you sell and the documents each one needs. These drive the checklist on every client's Documents tab.
  • + Add product or tap a product to edit it: Name, Icon, Colour, Required documents (tap the folder chips to choose) and the Active switch.
  • Long-press and drag to reorder.
  • Inactive products are hidden for new leads but kept on existing clients. This is safer than deleting.
  • The bin icon deletes a product (existing clients keep their records).

Default required documents — Loans and Mortgage: Passport, Emirates ID Front/Back, Visa, Salary Certificate, Bank Statement, Loan Application Form. Business Loan: Passport, Emirates ID Front/Back, Bank Statement, Company Trade License, Loan Application Form. Credit Card: as loans, but Credit Card Form instead of the loan form. Insurance: Passport, Emirates ID Front/Back.

23. Document categories

Document Categories
The folders in every client's vault.
  • + Add category creates a folder.
  • Drag the handle to reorder.
  • Row menu: Rename, Ask for expiry date / Stop asking for expiry, and Delete.
  • When you delete a folder its documents move to Other Documents. Nothing is deleted. Other Documents itself can't be removed.

24. Message templates

Message Templates
Template editor
Ready-made WhatsApp messages. The app ships with: Follow Up (default), Documents Required, Approved, Rejected, General Greeting.
  • + New template or menu Edit: enter a Template name and the Message. Tap a tag to insert it (for example {CustomerName} or {ProductName}). The tags are replaced with the client's details when you send.
  • Default template switch: the default is pre-selected in the WhatsApp sheet.
  • Preview shows a sample with tags filled in, and can be read aloud.
  • Menu: Duplicate, Set as default, Delete. Drag to reorder.

27. Security and security log

Security
Security log
  • Biometric unlock — fingerprint or face.
  • Change PIN — enter the current PIN, then the new one twice.
  • Pattern lock — draw a pattern of at least 4 dots, twice. Asks for your PIN.
  • Lock after inactivity — 1, 2, 5 (default), 10 or 30 minutes, or Never.
  • Regenerate recovery code — makes a new code. The old one stops working at once. Copy it and tap I saved it.
  • Security log — unlocks, failed attempts and intruder photos (tap one to see it full size).

28. Exporting data

Export data
  1. Choose the Period. Leads are included by the date they were created.
  2. Choose the Format: Excel workbook (.xlsx) (all leads plus approved, rejected, disbursed and pending-documents sheets) or PDF report (KPIs and summary tables).
  3. When it's ready, tap Share or Save to device….
Exports contain customer data. Share them only with people you trust.

29. Backup and restore

Backup & restore
Protects you if the phone is lost, reset or replaced. A backup contains all clients, followups, notes, settings and documents.
  • Backup now — makes a backup immediately. An automatic backup is also made every day, and the newest ones are kept.
  • Each backup's menu: Restore, Share, Save to device…, Delete.
  • Restore from file… — pick a backup .zip (for example one saved to Google Drive).
Restore replaces all current data on this phone. Unlock the app first.
Moving to a new phone: on the old phone tap Backup now, then Save to device… or Share to Google Drive. On the new phone, install the app, finish setup, then Restore from file….
The backup file contains customer documents in readable form. Keep it private.

30. Cloud sync

Cloud sync
Optional. It copies your data and documents to your own Firebase project, so a second phone can stay in step. Without it, the app works fully offline.
  • Status: Not configured, Turned off, Offline, Up to date, Syncing…, or Last sync failed.
  • Sync to cloud switch and Sync now. The result shows how many items were pushed, pulled and uploaded.
  • The chip on Home shows the same status. Tap it to retry.
  • Not configured means this copy of the app was built without Firebase settings. Ask whoever built your APK.

31. Sample data

Sample data
  • Load sample data — adds demo clients, followups, calls and documents so you can practise.
  • Clear sample data — removes only the demo clients and everything attached to them. Your real data is not touched.

32. About

About

Shows the version number (quote it when reporting a problem), the privacy statement and the open-source licences.

Privacy: all CRM data stays on this device, encrypted with AES-256. The key is kept in the Android Keystore and the app is protected by your PIN. Cloud sync, when you turn it on, copies data to your own Firebase project only.

33. Tips, FAQ and troubleshooting

A daily routine that works

  1. Unlock → check Today's tasks and Overdue on Home.
  2. Call from the task list. After each call, pick the outcome so the timeline and score stay accurate.
  3. Use Request on WhatsApp on the Documents tab for anything missing.
  4. When documents arrive on WhatsApp: Share → Anjali CRM AI, then pick the client and folder.
  5. Move the lead along with Change status, and schedule the next followup.
ProblemWhat to do
I forgot my PINLock screen → Forgot PIN? → enter your recovery code → set a new PIN.
I lost the recovery code tooThe data can't be opened without it (this protects your clients). Reinstall and restore from a backup file. Then go to Settings → Security → Regenerate recovery code and save the new one.
WhatsApp import shows no PDFsAndroid 11 and newer hide WhatsApp PDFs from other apps. Use Browse, or Share the file from WhatsApp to Anjali CRM AI.
"Only PDF, JPG, PNG and WEBP files can be saved"Ask the client to send a photo or PDF. Word and Excel files aren't stored in the vault.
Reminders don't arriveSettings → Notifications → Request notification permission. Also allow the app in the phone's battery settings (no restriction).
Dictation or voice search doesn't workAllow the microphone, and install or update Speech Services by Google.
Read-aloud has no voicesInstall Speech Services by Google, then choose a language in Voice & Speech.
Sync chip says "Sync Failed"Check the internet connection and tap the chip to retry. Your data stays safe on the phone either way.
"Unlock the app before restoring a backup"The vault was locked. Unlock, then restore again.
A document says "Could not open"The file may be damaged. Use Replace file to attach a fresh copy.
Phone lost or changedRestore from your latest backup (keep a copy off the phone, for example on Google Drive). If cloud sync was on, sign in on the new phone and sync.
Works offline: everything except WhatsApp, cloud sync and maps works without internet. Business-card scanning and OCR run on the phone.
