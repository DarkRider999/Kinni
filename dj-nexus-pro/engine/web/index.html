Try this

  • Press PLAY on A. On B, press SYNC, then PLAY: B locks to A's tempo and beat.
  • Turn B's LOW knob all the way down, raise the crossfader to the middle, then swap the bass.
  • Hold a ROLL size (slide across them to change it) or CENSOR, then let go: the deck lands back where the track would be.
  • Drag a jog wheel to scratch. Hold ‹ › to nudge.
  • Pick an FX, a beat length and hold HOLD, or tap ON. Try Echo on 1/2 beat and let go: it rings out.
  • Switch Color FX to Dub Echo or Space and twist a COLOR knob, then let it go: the tail rings on.
  • Press BUILD-UP (4 bars) while a deck plays: it sweeps, rolls faster and faster, then drops on the bar line.
  • Hit the sampler pads. Pads 13–16 are loops that follow the tempo; CAPTURE grabs beats from a deck.
  • Load your own MP3/WAV/FLAC or drop a file on a deck. The engine finds the BPM, the first downbeat and the key (Camelot and name); fix the BPM in the Grid box if needed.
  • Plug in a MIDI controller and press Connect, or send the test messages. LEARN binds any knob or button.

What's running

  • The same C++ engine as the app, compiled to WebAssembly and run on the browser's audio thread.
  • Key lock (tempo without pitch change), phase-locked sync, loops, slip, slip roll, censor, hot cues, isolator EQ, filter, limiter.
  • 12 beat FX timed from the master deck's beat grid, and a 16-voice sampler with quantized triggers and tempo-synced loops.
  • Six colour FX on the channel knobs and three bar-quantized macros: riser, build-up and drop.
  • MIDI mapping, learn, jog wheels and LED feedback, from the same engine code as the apps.
  • Demo tracks are generated in your browser. Nothing is uploaded; your files stay on this device.

Browser preview limits

  • Recording and headphone cue need the native app.
  • Loading a track pauses the audio for a moment.
  • BPM and key come from signal-processing analysis; trained AI models will replace them later.
  • Keys: Z/M play A/B, X/N cue, S/J sync, 1–8 pads, F hold FX 1.
